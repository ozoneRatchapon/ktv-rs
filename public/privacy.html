Privacy

KTV-RS · last updated 2026-09-27

In short

  • No accounts, no cookies of our own, no analytics or trackers.
  • Everything the app remembers stays in your browser (localStorage), not on a server.
  • The microphone is analysed inside this browser tab to show your pitch. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

What is stored on your device

KeyWhat for
ktv.settings.v1Your settings (intro skip, room name, timing tools, shortcut help seen)
ktv.session.v1Current song, queue, and songs you added by URL
ktv.picks.v1Favourites and recently sung songs
ktv.scores.v1Tuning scores of your last 20 songs (song, notes held, average cents off)
ktv.guides.v1Guide timings you saved in Guide Timing Tools
_ktv_*_leadHow long this device's players take to start, so the original-vocal guide stays in sync

Remove all of it at any time: Settings → Clear my data on this device. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

Third parties

  • YouTube (Google): videos play in YouTube's embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Inside those players Google's privacy policy applies, and YouTube may store data in your browser once a video plays. Song thumbnails load from i.ytimg.com.
  • Cloudflare serves the app's files. Like any web host it sees your IP address to deliver them; we have not added any logging or analytics of our own.
  • GitHub: only if you use Share on GitHub in Guide Timing Tools, which opens a pre-filled issue in your browser. Nothing is sent until you submit it there.

Fonts

Fonts (Kanit, Outfit) are served from this site, not from Google Fonts.
