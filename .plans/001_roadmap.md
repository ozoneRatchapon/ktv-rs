## Out of scope / constraints (by design)
- No re-hosting or downloading YouTube audio (Developer Policies III.E.1.a), no hidden/background players or audio separation (III.I.7/III.I.9), no overlays on players (Required Minimum Functionality). App complies since 2026-09-26.
- Guide timing without downloads (owner decision 2026-09-26): `tools/guide_align.py` (yt-dlp audio download, against YouTube's terms) removed; guides are timed by ear in the app (Guide Timing Tools). Existing `guide` numbers in the catalog stay.
- Commercial/venue use needs Thai public-performance licences regardless of the app.
- Backing-track pitch shift is impossible with iframe embeds; only achievable with licensed own audio.
